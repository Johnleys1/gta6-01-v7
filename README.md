Aurora Falls — a snowbound 3D open-world crime game. Kai, a getaway driver, takes on THE WARDEN across five districts of a mountain city under the aurora: 8 staged missions, AFPD dispatch with helicopter NORTHERN-1, roadblocks and spike strips, ice-road driving with drift, a 4-weapon wheel, exploding cars and persistent burning wrecks, garage upgrades, district standing, respect ranks, street races, taxi and delivery jobs, saves, and 3 procedural radio stations.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server --directory game 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Drive with WASD, drift with Shift, E to enter/steal cars, click to shoot, 1–4 to switch weapons, Q for the weapon wheel, R for radio, G at the garage, J for a side job.
